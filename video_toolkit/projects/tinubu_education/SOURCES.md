# Figures used in the education video, and where they come from

| On screen | Claim | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ₦375bn, 1.67 million loans | NELFUND cumulative student-loan disbursement N375.28bn (N194.77bn fees + N180.51bn upkeep), 1,675,474 loans, 320 institutions | NELFUND disbursement status report, 9 Nov 2026 (Nairametrics 9 Nov 2026; Punch 10 Nov 2026) |
| 1 million+ | Out-of-school children returned to learning (BESDA), 17 northern states | Federal Ministry of Education NESRI 2026 report, quoted by APC PCC statement 4 Nov 2026 (Punch, Vanguard, Premium Times) |
| 156,446 | Verified TVET trainees on free training with monthly stipends and start-up support | Same NESRI 2026 report (BusinessDay 5 Nov 2026) |

"Interest-free": NELFUND loans carry no interest (Students Loans (Access to Higher Education) Act 2024).
The 1m+ and 156,446 figures are government-reported, not independently audited. Update the NELFUND
figure before airing if a newer report is out (`make_slides.py`, `SLIDES` and `TILES`).

Illustrative people (Ngozi, Aisha, Tunde) are AI-generated images; the end card's AI disclosure covers them.
